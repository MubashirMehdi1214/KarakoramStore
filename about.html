About Us

Bringing the best of Gilgit-Baltistan and the Karakoram to your doorstep.

KarakoramStore is an online shop dedicated to authentic products from Pakistan’s northern mountains. We work with trusted local suppliers to offer genuine Gilgiti caps, pure Himalayan shilajit and other regional specialties.

What we offer

Our collection focuses on quality, authenticity and fair sourcing. Every product is selected for its cultural value, craftsmanship and real benefit to our customers — whether you want traditional wear or natural wellness from the Karakoram.

How to order

Browse products by category, read full descriptions and prices on each product page, then contact us by phone or WhatsApp to confirm your order. We arrange delivery across Pakistan with clear communication at every step.

Our promise

We stand behind what we sell. If you have questions about a product, delivery or your order, reach out through our contact page and we will respond as quickly as possible.
